I'm a founding principal AI researcher at [Rhizome Labs](https://rhizome-labs.com), working on next generation deep learning approaches. Most recently I have begun work on deep equilibrium models (DEQs). Other recent research interests including Bayesian deep learning (BDL), diffusion models, and probabilistic modeling, and I've made significant contributions in each of these areas. 

<br>

In particular, I'm interested in using DEQs to improve deep learning and foundation model efficiency. I have additional research interests in scalable stochastic gradient Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) approaches from BDL to enable uncertainty quantification, improve data efficiency, and reduce hallucinations in foundation models. I'm also interested in diffusion-based approaches for time series foundation models, and in domain adaptation for foundation models.

<br>

Previously I was as a lead AI research scientist at [Sigma Nova](https://www.sigmanova.ai) in Paris, France, where I worked on generative AI topics for scientific foundation models. Prior to Sigma Nova, I worked as a senior researcher at the [Criteo AI Lab](https://ailab.criteo.com) in Paris, where I pursued research on generative AI, recommender systems, and a class of models for subset selection known as determinantal point processes (DPPs). And prior to Criteo I worked as a post-doc researcher at [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/), where I focused on recommender systems and DPPs. I received my PhD in 2014 from the University of Colorado Boulder, where I worked on recommender systems and was supervised by [Richard Han](https://researchers.mq.edu.au/en/persons/richard-han).
